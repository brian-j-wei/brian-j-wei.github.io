Research

I am interested in multimodal models, representation learning, controllable image and video generation, and developing efficient approaches for large-scale models. My work focuses on applications in remote sensing and natural image domains, and I am also eager to explore a broader range of topics.

TerraDiT: Point-Conditioned Diffusion Transformer for Satellite Image Synthesis
Srikumar Sastry*, Dan Cher*, Brian Wei*, Aayush Dhakal, Subash Khanal, Dev Gupta, Nathan Jacobs
TerraBytes II, ECCV, 2026 (Oral)
